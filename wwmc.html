🕘 Activity log

Every change on the site — who did it and when: tickets, daily reports, documents, users, approvals and the O&M Library. Entries can't be edited.

🗂 File storage

Documents and photos are now saved in Supabase Storage, which keeps the database small and the site fast. Older files may still be inside the database — move them here. Each file is uploaded and checked before anything in the database changes; you can stop and continue later.

🛟 Backups

Full backup — one ZIP with everything: all data plus every photo and document as normal files you can open. Nothing is changed or deleted. Keep it on your computer or a drive (it includes user accounts — store it safely).

Automatic copy — every night at 02:30 (Riyadh) all QAS2 data is copied inside Supabase. Copies are kept, never deleted.
